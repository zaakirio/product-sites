Help with Vellichor

Vellichor is a private reader and on-device narrator for EPUB, PDF, and plain-text documents. If something is not working, write to us and we will help.

Importing a book

Open the Library, use the add button, and choose an EPUB, PDF, or text file through Apple’s document picker. Some scanned or unusually formatted PDFs may not keep their original structure.

Narration

Narration is prepared on your device, so the first sentence of a new book can take a moment while the app gets ahead of you. Tap a paragraph to start listening from that point, and choose a narrator or playback speed from the player.

Purchases

The lifetime unlock is a non-consumable App Store purchase. If an earlier purchase is not recognised, open the purchase screen and choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Apple manages charges, receipts, and refunds. Use reportaproblem.apple.com for billing and refund requests.

Deleting local data

Books and prepared audio are stored in Vellichor’s app container. Delete an individual book through its actions in the Library. Deleting Vellichor removes its local app data according to iOS behaviour.

For what the app stores and why, read the Vellichor Privacy Policy.

Copyright

Vellichor does not provide books. Import only documents you are authorised to access and use.
